RecoveryAI - Autonomous B2B Payment Recovery

The authoritative, comprehensive guide to the RecoveryAI autonomous recovery engine, deterministic state machine, bounded concession calculator, multilingual voice pipeline, and Supabase PostgreSQL persistence layer.

1. Executive Overview

In modern commerce, when an invoice or recurring debit fails due to an expired mandate, card timeout, or temporary liquidity crunch, traditional financial tools stop at a single dead end: PAYMENT FAILED. The debt is dumped into a spreadsheet for manual follow-up weeks later, resulting in high write-offs, margin bleed, and broken customer relationships.

RecoveryAI solves this by closing the autonomous revenue recovery loop:

1. Detect & Ingest

Real-time webhook / API ingestion

Instantly captures the failed payment event (e.g. ₹45,000 via UPI Autopay) and loads customer risk profile and merchant policy.

2. Diagnose & Schedule

Root-cause classification

Identifies whether the failure was technical (gateway timeout) or liquidity-driven, generating personalized WhatsApp reminders and scheduling exact follow-ups.

3. Bounded Negotiation

Anti-gaming concession engine

Places natural Hinglish/English outbound AI voice calls to negotiate promises-to-pay (PTP) or step through strict merchant-capped discount tiers (5% → 8% → 10%).

4. Reconcile or Escalate

Deterministic FSM settlement

Dispatches payment links, confirms settlements, freezes disputes for human finance review, or escalates chronic refusals to senior officers.

2. RecoveryAI in 5 Minutes (Non-Technical Explanation)

Imagine a tireless, senior credit officer who monitors every single invoice across your business 24/7. When a customer’s payment fails:

  1. Memory: She checks who the customer is, their lifetime spend (LTV), and whether they have a habit of asking for discounts every month.
  2. Diagnosis: She figures out why it failed—was it a temporary bank server glitch, or does the customer not have enough cash today?
  3. Timing: Instead of spamming them immediately, she waits the exact right number of minutes before sending a polite WhatsApp message with a direct UPI payment link.
  4. Voice Call: If no payment arrives, she calls the debtor in fluent, natural conversational Hinglish or English to discuss the invoice.
  5. Strict Rules: If the customer asks for a discount, she does not invent random numbers. She follows the merchant’s hard rules: offering 5% first, 8% next, and stopping completely if the customer has abused discounts before.
  6. Escalation: If the customer disputes the bill amount (e.g. GST error), she immediately pauses outreach and routes the dossier to human finance.
The Golden Principle
RecoveryAI prioritizes collecting 100% of the invoice first. It only sacrifices margin when customer behavior and merchant policies strictly justify it, preventing opportunistic debtors from gaming the system.

3. The Complete Current Pipeline

Below is the exact architectural pipeline implemented in RecoveryAI, spanning the FastAPI backend, Gemini 3.6 Flash intent layer, Sarvam AI speech synthesis, and PostgreSQL database:

1
Payment Failure / Invoice Ingestion
Invoice record created with status UNPAID, state TRIGGERED, and next_action_due_at = now + 10 min.
2
Profile Lookup & Root-Cause Diagnosis
Loads Customer (LTV, consecutive discount months) and Merchant (default discount cap). Categorizes failure reason (GATEWAY_TIMEOUT, INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS, MANDATE_DECLINE, etc.).
3
Autonomous WhatsApp / SMS Reminder Dispatch
Scheduler fires when deadline expires → calls Gemini to generate empathetic dunning copy → transitions to REMINDER_SENT → schedules next 10-minute timer.
4
Call Queue Enqueueing
If reminder deadline expires without payment, scheduler sets call_pending = true, inserting the invoice into the live global voice call queue.
5
Outbound Voice Interaction (Sarvam + Gemini)
Sarvam bulbul-v3 plays spoken opening greeting. Debtor replies via audio/text. Sarvam saaras-v3 transcribes speech, and Gemini parses debtor intent.
6
Deterministic Concession Ladder / State Transition
Based on intent: PTP commitment recorded (PTP_ACTIVE), billing dispute frozen (FROZEN_DISPUTE), payment link dispatched (LINK_SENT), or discount ladder climbed (TIER_1_DISCOUNT → TIER_2_DISCOUNT → TIER_3_FLOOR).
7
Settlement Reconciliation & Audit Appending
Invoice marked RESOLVED upon payment. An immutable audit record is appended to recovery_events with exact financial and conversational rationale.

5. The Agent Brain Architecture & Guardrails

A core design flaw in naive AI agents is granting an LLM direct, unconstrained authority to modify database balances or invent discount percentages. In RecoveryAI, the architecture enforces strict separation of concerns:

A
1. LLM Cognitive Layer (Gemini 3.6 Flash)
Role: Natural language understanding and copy generation ONLY. Classifies intent from transcripts, extracts proposed dates, and crafts conversational Hinglish text. Has ZERO authority to mutate database state or approve discounts.
B
2. Deterministic Decision Engine
Role: Pure Python mathematical logic. Evaluates risk profile, root-cause compatibility, historical recovery rates, and selects candidate actions.
C
3. Policy Engine & Anti-Gaming Calculator
Role: Enforces merchant caps and customer historical penalties. Clamps discount rates, blocks higher tiers for repeat exploiters, and ensures margin preservation.
D
4. Finite State Machine (FSM) & Persistence
Role: Validates transitions against a strict directional graph. Appends immutable records to PostgreSQL and syncs invoice status.

6. Deterministic Decision Engine & Scoring

When an invoice failure is diagnosed, RecoveryAI evaluates candidate actions based on root-cause category, customer LTV, and historical reliability:

Failure Reason Primary Diagnostic Action Business & Operational Rationale
GATEWAY_TIMEOUT SEND_LINK Technical failure at card/bank gateway. Customer has funds; immediate alternate link resolves case with 0% margin loss.
INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS SET_PTP Liquidity timing mismatch. Offering immediate discounts rewards poor planning; securing a 3-day promise-to-pay recovers 100% principal.
MANDATE_DECLINE OFFER_DISCOUNT Recurring autopay mandate expired. Friction to re-authenticate is high; small concession incentivizes immediate manual settlement.
EXPIRED_CARD SEND_LINK Card expired. Customer requires update-card portal + alternate UPI link.
DISPUTED_AMOUNT FLAG_DISPUTE Billing / GST discrepancy. Automated recovery is paused immediately to prevent customer alienation.

8. Customer Historical Signals

Every customer record in the customers table maintains vital financial telemetry used by the engine to balance customer retention against margin preservation:

Customer Lifetime Value (ltv_inr)

Type: NUMERIC(12, 2)

Total historical revenue collected from this debtor. High LTV accounts (e.g. Ananya Iyer, ₹2,10,000) are routed directly to dedicated senior officers when disputed rather than facing automated pressure.

Consecutive Discount Months (consecutive_discount_months)

Type: INTEGER (Default 0)

The primary anti-gaming counter. Increments whenever a debtor resolves an invoice via a concession tier. Automatically penalizes effective discount caps on subsequent invoices.

Primary Contact Telemetry (phone, email)

Type: VARCHAR(20), VARCHAR(255)

Normalized Indian mobile format (+9198XXXXXXXX) used for automated WhatsApp dunning dispatches, SMS payment link generation, and Sarvam outbound voice dialing.

9. Merchant Policy Configuration

Merchants retain sovereign control over recovery boundaries via the merchants table:

  • default_discount_cap (NUMERIC 5,4): Defines the ceiling ratio of revenue the merchant is willing to sacrifice to resolve delinquent invoices (e.g. 0.1000 = 10%).
  • Cascade Isolation: All invoices and customers belong to a foreign-key-backed merchant_id. Overrides or policy changes apply strictly to the merchant's portfolio without data contamination across tenants.

10. Deterministic Finite State Machine (FSM)

The recovery lifecycle is governed by a formal, deterministic state machine implemented in app.engine.state_machine.StateMachine. Past events are strictly immutable; every state transition appends a new row to recovery_events.

State Category Operational Meaning Allowed Next States
TRIGGERED Active Payment failure ingested; initial 10m countdown active before reminder. REMINDER_SENT, PTP_ACTIVE, TIER_1_DISCOUNT, LINK_SENT, ESCALATED_HUMAN
DIAGNOSED Legacy/Audit Diagnosis logged inside the unified reminder dispatch step. REMINDER_SENT, LINK_SENT, TIER_1_DISCOUNT, PTP_ACTIVE
REMINDER_SENT Active WhatsApp/SMS reminder dispatched; 10m countdown active before voice call queueing. PTP_ACTIVE, LINK_SENT, TIER_1_DISCOUNT, ESCALATED_HUMAN, RESOLVED
LINK_SENT Active Alternate UPI/Card payment link dispatched with 1-hour settlement window. RESOLVED, TIER_1_DISCOUNT, PTP_ACTIVE, ESCALATED_HUMAN
PTP_ACTIVE Active (Paused) Debtor committed to pay by a specific date (≤ 3 days). Outreach paused until deadline. RESOLVED, LINK_SENT, TIER_1_DISCOUNT, ESCALATED_HUMAN
TIER_1_DISCOUNT Concession 50% of merchant cap offered (e.g. 5% waiver). Awaiting settlement. RESOLVED, TIER_2_DISCOUNT, PTP_ACTIVE, ESCALATED_HUMAN
TIER_2_DISCOUNT Concession 80% of merchant cap offered (e.g. 8% waiver). Awaiting settlement. RESOLVED, TIER_3_FLOOR, PTP_ACTIVE, ESCALATED_HUMAN
TIER_3_FLOOR Concession 100% of merchant cap offered (e.g. 10% maximum waiver). Final automated offer. RESOLVED, ESCALATED_HUMAN, PTP_ACTIVE
RESOLVED Terminal Payment fully collected and reconciled. Workflow complete. None (Terminal)
FROZEN_DISPUTE Terminal Debtor flagged billing error. Outreach permanently paused for human audit. None (Terminal)
ESCALATED_HUMAN Terminal Debtor refused all options or policy limit reached. Assigned to human officer. None (Terminal)
Global Escape Hatches
Any active state can transition globally to FROZEN_DISPUTE (if customer signals a billing dispute) or RESOLVED (if an external payment webhook arrives).

11. Autonomous Scheduler & Background Workers

Autonomy in RecoveryAI is driven by app.scheduler.run_scheduler(), an asynchronous background task registered within FastAPI's lifespan lifecycle in main.py: 

# Background Worker Loop in app/scheduler.py
POLL_INTERVAL_SECONDS = 20

async def run_scheduler() -> None:
    logger.info("🤖 RecoveryAI autonomous scheduler started (poll=20s)")
    while True:
        await asyncio.sleep(POLL_INTERVAL_SECONDS)
        try:
            result = await process_expired_deadlines()
            if result["fired"] or result["calls_queued"]:
                logger.info("⚙️ Scheduler cycle: %s", result)
        except Exception as e:
            logger.error("Scheduler cycle error: %s", e)Python / FastAPI

The scheduler queries the database for all unpaid invoices where next_action_due_at <= now() and call_pending == false:

  • TRIGGERED: Automatically generates personalized dunning copy via Gemini, transitions the invoice to REMINDER_SENT, and sets a 10-minute timer for voice call eligibility.
  • REMINDER_SENT / LINK_SENT: When the 10-minute reminder window expires without payment, the scheduler sets call_pending = true and clears the timer, signaling the frontend to enqueue a voice call.
  • PTP_ACTIVE: If the debtor breaches their promise-to-pay deadline, the scheduler directly queues an outbound voice call to re-negotiate or escalate.
  • TIER_3_FLOOR: If the final floor discount window expires without payment, the scheduler transitions the invoice to ESCALATED_HUMAN.

12. Real-Time Countdown Engine (Server Truth vs UI Display)

A frequent architectural pitfall in web applications is storing countdown timers in React component state, which resets whenever a user refreshes their browser.

RecoveryAI strictly separates Server Truth from UI Display:

1. Server Truth (PostgreSQL)

invoices.next_action_due_at

Stored as an exact UTC timestamp (TIMESTAMPTZ). Remains completely invariant regardless of browser refreshes, tab closures, or network reconnects.

2. UI Display (React page.tsx)

formatRemainingTime(target, now)

A client-side setInterval ticks every 1 second, calculating targetDate.getTime() - now.getTime(). When the countdown reaches 00:00, the UI triggers background sync.

13. Fast-Forward & Simulation Subsystem

To allow operators and demonstration viewers to test multi-day recovery lifecycles in seconds without waiting 10 minutes or 3 days, RecoveryAI provides the /api/simulation/fast-forward and /api/invoices/{id}/skip-wait endpoints.

Architectural Integrity of Fast Forward
Fast Forward does NOT bypass the state machine or force arbitrary states. Instead, it mathematically winds back stored deadlines in PostgreSQL (e.g. next_action_due_at = now - 1s) and passes execution through the exact same authoritative process_expired_deadlines() scheduler logic.

14. Call Queue Subsystem

When an invoice reaches an actionable calling state, it enters the global calling queue:

  • Eligibility: An invoice is call-eligible if call_pending == true or if an operator triggers Skip Wait / Simulate Breach.
  • Single-Focus Drawer: The frontend CallQueueDrawer manages active calls sequentially. Only one call modal can be active at a time to prevent audio playback overlap.
  • Acknowledgment: Upon call completion or auto-close, the frontend calls POST /api/invoices/{id}/acknowledge-call to reset call_pending = false in PostgreSQL.

17. Sarvam AI Speech Integration & Audio Caching

Implemented in app.engine.sarvam_service. Provides speech capabilities:

  • Speech-to-Text: saaras:v3 model via https://api.sarvam.ai/speech-to-text with strict 2.5s network timeout.
  • Text-to-Speech: bulbul:v3 model with speaker="shubh" and target_language_code="hi-IN".
  • In-Memory SHA-256 Audio Cache: Common greetings and concession statements are cached in _TTS_AUDIO_CACHE, serving repeated speech requests in 0.15ms.

27. Manual Operator Overrides

Human operators can intervene at any point via the Override menu in the dashboard. Available actions:

  • MANUAL_LINK: Dispatches an immediate payment link (transitions to LINK_SENT).
  • SIMULATE_PTP: Manually records a 3-day promise-to-pay commitment (PTP_ACTIVE).
  • FORCE_DISCOUNT: Overrides policy to offer Tier 1 discount immediately (TIER_1_DISCOUNT).
  • FLAG_DISPUTE: Freezes case for billing audit (FROZEN_DISPUTE).
  • MARK_SETTLED: Confirms off-platform bank transfer/cash payment (RESOLVED).
  • ESCALATE_HUMAN: Routes dossier to senior credit officer (ESCALATED_HUMAN).

29. Analytics & Preserved Margin

The Analytics Engine calculates live portfolio health across 4 key metrics:

Total Volume at Risk

Gross INR volume across all delinquent invoices ingested into the pipeline.

Gross Recovered

Total principal collected across resolved cases before concessions.

Anti-Gaming Margin Preserved

Calculated as Gross Amount × (Merchant Cap − Discount Granted). Measures exact INR revenue saved by withholding excessive discounts.

Recovery Win Rate

(Gross Recovered / Total Volume at Risk) × 100.

34. Practical Troubleshooting

Problem: Frontend shows empty table

Resolution

Click the Seed Database button on the top right of the dashboard to trigger POST /api/seed.

Problem: Countdown frozen at 00:00

Resolution

The scheduler processes expired deadlines every 20 seconds. You can click Skip Wait on the invoice to immediately trigger the next action.

Problem: Voice call modal has no sound

Resolution

Browser autoplay policies may block audio. Click the Replay button on the speech bubble to initiate audio playback.

35. Technical & Business Glossary

Term Plain-English Definition
LTV (Lifetime Value) The total revenue a customer has historically paid to the merchant. Used to protect VIP accounts from harsh collection tactics.
PTP (Promise to Pay) A formal commitment made by a debtor to clear their overdue invoice by an agreed future date (maximum 3 days under standard policy).
Concession Ladder A stepped sequence of discounts (Tier 1 = 5%, Tier 2 = 8%, Tier 3 = 10%) offered only when debtors refuse full-price settlement.
Anti-Gaming Rules that detect and penalize chronic discount demanders by clamping or eliminating discount eligibility on subsequent invoices.
Margin Preserved The exact amount of revenue saved by resolving invoices without granting the merchant's maximum permissible discount.
FSM (Finite State Machine) A mathematical model of states and transitions ensuring an invoice can only move through approved, predictable recovery stages.
