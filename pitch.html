06 • Execution Pipeline

The Exact 5-Stage Recovery Pipeline

From automated webhook trigger to conversational voice negotiation, PTP tracking, and ledger settlement.

Stage 01

Ingestion & Diagnosis

Invoice failure ingested via webhook or batch seed. Case initialized in TRIGGERED state and diagnosed for root cause (e.g. Mandate Decline).

Stage 02

Autonomous Dunning

Multi-channel WhatsApp/SMS payment link dispatched. State advances to REMINDER_SENT with a 10-minute autonomous countdown timer.

Stage 03

Outbound Voice Call

Scheduler expires reminder timer and queues call. Sarvam STT transcribes Hinglish speech; Gemini extracts non-authoritative intent.

Stage 04

Policy & Split Engine

Deterministic calculator evaluates concession rules, enforces split plans (1-hr window), or binds authorized discount tiers (Tier 1 → 2 → 3).

Stage 05

Settlement & Audit

Customer pays via link or records 2-stage partial payment. Status updates to RESOLVED with full immutable audit logging.

Autonomous Worker Background Scheduler runs every 20 seconds: monitors 10m reminder countdowns, 1h split deadlines, and 3-day PTP breaches.
